Why Choose a PGA Professional?

Most drone operators know cameras. I know golf. As a PGA professional based in Hamburg, I understand what makes a hole look challenging, what golfers want to see before they play, and how to frame a course in a way that makes it look its best.

FAQ

Does the course need to be closed during filming?
No. We only need 3 consecutive tee times blocked from 8am. We follow normal pace of play and won't disrupt your members.
What areas in Germany do you cover?
Based in Hamburg, we cover the whole of northern Germany and travel throughout Germany for the right project. Travel costs may apply outside the Hamburg area.
How long until we receive the finished videos?
You'll receive your first draft within 7 days of the shoot date. One round of revisions is included.
What format are the videos delivered in?
MP4, optimised for web and social media. We also provide 9:16 vertical cuts (for Instagram Reels and TikTok) in the Premium package.
Do you have the required drone licence?
Yes. We fly fully compliant with EU drone regulations, registered with the German Luftfahrt-Bundesamt (LBA) and operating under the A1 open category.
